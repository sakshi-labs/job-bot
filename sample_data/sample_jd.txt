Senior Backend Infrastructure Engineer
Company: CloudScale AI
Location: San Francisco, CA / Remote

About the Role:
CloudScale AI is building the next-generation real-time ML feature serving platform. We are looking for an experienced Senior Backend Infrastructure Engineer to lead the design and implementation of our distributed streaming architecture.

Responsibilities:
- Architect and scale low-latency event-driven microservices processing billions of events per day.
- Manage and optimize large-scale Apache Kafka clusters, schema registries, and consumer group rebalances.
- Design resilient storage backends using PostgreSQL, Redis, and DynamoDB.
- Deploy and orchestrate services on AWS (EKS / Kubernetes) using Terraform and CI/CD automation.
- Drive observability improvements across microservices using OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, and Grafana.
- Collaborate with Machine Learning Platform teams to reduce end-to-end inference latencies.

Qualifications:
- 5+ years of software engineering experience in Go, Java, or Python.
- Deep hands-on experience with Apache Kafka, distributed systems, and stream processing.
- Proven experience deploying containerized applications with Docker and Kubernetes in AWS.
- Strong knowledge of relational and NoSQL databases (PostgreSQL, Redis).
- Experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform) and modern CI/CD pipelines.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
